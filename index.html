Biography

I am an M.Sc. student in Electrical Engineering at the National University of Singapore, working on Robot Learning at Show Lab, advised by Prof. Mike Zheng Shou. My research interests include Generalist Robot Policies, Embodied Agents, and Diffusion Models.

I am also an AI Scientist Intern at Mikomiko, working on agent-powered image generation and editing models.

Previously, I received my B.Eng. in Automation from Zhejiang University, where my early research focused on control in robot systems.
