Biography

I am an M.Sc. student in Electrical Engineering at the National University of Singapore, working on Robot Learning at Show Lab under the supervision of Prof. Mike Zheng Shou. My research interests include Vision-Language-Action Models, World Models, and Diffusion Models.

Previously, I received my B.Eng. in Automation from Zhejiang University, where my early research focused on robot control.
